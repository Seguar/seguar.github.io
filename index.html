mm-wave arrays · RFSoC · radar

Seguar

I’m a PhD candidate working on hardware for large E-band massive MIMO arrays: how to distribute LO, clock and baseband across a tiled aperture, and how to calibrate it with over-the-air measurement plus built-in self-test. Hands-on, that means RFSoC boards: direction-of-arrival and beamforming experiments, phase calibration and multi-tile synchronization. Below are browser tools I built for sizing and comparing RF hardware, and my code.

    Hardware map and live model costing every LO and baseband option for a 71–86 GHz array, drawn on the floorplan of a 30 cm, 5×5-tile aperture.

    Open tool: E-Band Distribution Comparison Hardware map of the E-band tool Recorded decision in the E-band tool

    • LO distribution

    • Massive MIMO

    An architecture-selection instrument, not a validated simulator. Every default is labelled datasheet, literature, scaled or guess.
