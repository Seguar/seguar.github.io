  • Can several ESP32-C5 chips share one external 48 MHz reference (XTAL_P driven by an active oscillator / clock buffer) and keep a deterministic LO phase on the 5 GHz path after PLL relock and channel change? This needs bench testing.
  • Has ESP-SDR raw I/Q capture on the C5 been validated at 5.18-5.24 GHz (U-NII-1)? Measure the actual 5 GHz RX tuning range, noise figure, image/DC behaviour and maximum burst length (C5 chip.h defines IQ_WORDS 16380; samples per word not verified).
  • Is ESP-SDR's 8/10-bit burst capture time-alignable across 16 chips (trigger/timestamp mechanism)? It is not documented in the README.
  • Does the C5 5 GHz front end (ANT_5G) support RX at 40 MHz analog bandwidth in raw-capture mode? ESP-SDR states 11-48 MHz, but whether this is band-specific is not documented.
  • The Espressif Product Selector could not be read (JavaScript-only). Per-part lifecycle status should be checked manually for the chosen C5 part (e.g., ESP32-C5HF4 vs HR2/HR8).

Open questions

  • PARLIO, SDIO-slave and GP-SPI timing details (setup/hold, external-clock max for PARLIO RX, DMA burst bandwidth) still need checking against the TRM chapters.
  • Sustained PARLIO RX/TX throughput is untested: can 8-bit x 40 MHz (320 Mbit/s) actually flow through GDMA into 384 KB SRAM (or into PSRAM) while Wi-Fi is active? No Espressif benchmark exists; this needs a bench test.
  • Espressif publishes no ESP32-C5 SPI-slave half-duplex quad (SPI-HD) throughput figure. The 40 MHz x 4-line raw ceiling of 160 Mbit/s is unverified.
  • The LP CPU maximum frequency is inconsistent: datasheet v1.5 says up to 48 MHz, while the 2025-04-30 mass-production press release says up to 40 MHz.
  • In the pdftotext extraction of Table 1-1, the chip revision per part number (v1.0 vs v1.2 for HR8/HF4) is ambiguous. This must be checked on the rendered PDF and the chip marking, because erratum CPU-718 (PSRAM) applies to v1.0.
  • Can the XTAL_P/N input be driven by a shared external 48 MHz reference for all 16 chips (pin description says 'crystal or oscillator')? Whether the 5 GHz RF PLL/LO phase is deterministic across resets or channel switches is undocumented; this is the key ESPARGOS-style phase-coherence question.
  • The 5 GHz TX power and sensitivity variation across UNII sub-bands (5180-5885 MHz) and over temperature is not given; only typical values at 25 C are listed.
  • The on-module diplexer insertion loss and the 2.4/5 GHz isolation are not specified. For a 5 GHz-only array, a custom PCB could route ANT_5G directly and skip the diplexer; the gain from doing so is unquantified.
  • The ESP-Hosted numbers are from open-air tests with an ESP32-P4 host. Results with an FPGA or other host, or at 50 MHz SDIO, are unknown.

Open questions

  • Can the C5 sample-dump engine run in a continuous mode, for example the C6/C61-style two-bank rotation? The firmware notes that the continuous-gate bit gave only a short, incomplete snapshot on the C5, and nothing else is documented.
  • Can the C5 modem diagnostic bus be routed through the GPIO matrix to PARLIO or SPI, as on the S31? If so, what sample rate and bit depth fit within the C5's 8-bit, 40 MHz PARLIO limit?
  • Are the C5's 20/10/8/4 MS/s rates filtered decimation or subsampling? How much aliasing results, given the C5's minimum analog bandwidth of about 11 MHz?
  • Which 10-bit field is I and which is Q in raw C5 CAP words, and what is the spectral sign? ESPARGOS sources disagree, so this needs a bench test with a known tone.
  • How repeatable is the C5 LO phase across resets, retunes, 2.4/5 GHz path switches and repeated adctrig() calls when all chips share one 48 MHz reference? Does the sample-clock alignment re-roll on every capture, as ESPARGOS One's sample engine does on every sync, retune or sample-rate change?
  • What is the latency and jitter from a software or GPIO-interrupt-initiated adctrig() to the first sample on the C5? Can 16 boards start captures within one sample (12.5 ns at 80 MS/s) without ESPARGOS's closed-firmware timestamp anchoring?
  • What snapshot rate (captures per second) does the C5 actually achieve over USB Serial/JTAG and over 2 Mbaud UART? No measured C5 figure is published.
  • How does the C5 perform at 5 GHz: sensitivity or noise figure, gain table in dB, image rejection across 5150-5895 MHz, and reception with a known signal above 5250 MHz?
  • Do boot-time and background PHY calibration tones from 16 nodes create a regulatory or self-interference problem, and can they be suppressed?
  • Will the ESPARGOS One sensor firmware's IQ, sync and trigger mechanisms be open-sourced or ported to the ESP32-C5 at 5 GHz?
  • Does esp_phy_wifi_tx_tone, or any documented API, produce a usable 5 GHz CW calibration reference on the C5? Will the authors' withheld TX path ever be released?

Open questions

  • What is the allowed XTAL_P input amplitude range on the ESP32-C5 (min and max Vpp)? Is a 3.3 V LVCMOS square wave through a DC-blocking cap safe, or must it be attenuated or clipped-sine? The forum's unverified 1.1 V limit needs bench or Espressif confirmation.
  • Does the C5 need any register or eFuse setting to bypass or disable the internal crystal amplifier when driven by an external clock, or does the S2-style 'drive XTAL_P, float XTAL_N' simply work?
  • Which coupling element suits the C5: the S2-style ~50 pF DC-blocking capacitor, the legacy C3-style ~20 nH series inductor, or both? What is the C5 XTAL_P input impedance at 48 MHz?
  • What are the C5 RF synthesizer's loop bandwidth, PFD frequency and architecture? Measure LO phase noise in TX-tone mode with a clean versus a degraded reference to find the crossover where reference noise stops dominating.
  • Does the closed C5 Wi-Fi PHY support a 40 MHz crystal, given that ESP-IDF allows 40/48 MHz autodetect via MTMS and eFuse but the HDG says 48 MHz only? This matters for sharing a reference with ESPARGOS-style 40 MHz hardware.
  • Does the C5 PLL show the same random starting phase after reset or channel change that ESPARGOS observed on the ESP32-S2? How often must the 5 GHz phase reference be re-injected?
  • What is the temperature coefficient of buffer output skew (ps/°C) for the candidate buffers? It is not given in the LMK1C1104, CDCLVC1310, Si53302 or 5PB1104 datasheets, but it sets phase drift at 5 GHz (1 ps ≈ 1.9°).
  • Do harmonics of a 48 MHz square-wave distribution network (e.g., 5184 and 5232 MHz) create in-band spurs or desense at the C5 receivers? Is slew-rate limiting or clipped-sine distribution needed?
  • What circuit does ESPARGOS use to inject the 40 MHz clock at each ESP32 (buffer part, level, coupling)? It is not published; ask the authors or inspect hardware.
  • Does the chip's internal glitch filter on the main clock (mentioned in the datasheet) affect jitter transfer or duty-cycle sensitivity with an external square-wave reference?
